# Contributor licence

[Wersja polska](CONTRIBUTOR-LICENCE.pl.md)

The document governing rights in contributions made to Open Radio. It
complements `CONTRIBUTING.md`, which covers the technical side. **The Polish
version governs**; this English text is informative, because the agreement is
between contributors and a Polish maintainer under Polish law.

## Why this exists

Open Radio has a single maintainer and is released under GPL-3.0-or-later. The
project may in future also be offered under terms alongside the GPL — for
example a commercial licence for someone who cannot use copyleft.

For that to stay possible, the maintainer needs the right to grant further
licences in every contributor's work. Without it, one change made years ago by
someone who can no longer be found blocks the whole project. This document
settles that once, up front and openly, instead of as an awkward question asked
individually and too late.

## Forming the agreement

Submitting a contribution — a pull request, a patch, a documentation fix,
catalog data or artwork — forms a licence agreement on the terms of this
document, between you as Licensor and Tomasz Fiedoruk as Licensee. You confirm
it with the `Signed-off-by:` trailer required by `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

The licence is non-exclusive, so it does not require written form under Polish
law.

These fields cover the forms of exploitation known on the day the contribution
is made.

that is, to disposing of and using adaptations of it within the fields of
exploitation above — and to that consent being passed on further.

Without this clause the Licensee could use your contribution but could not
effectively allow anyone else to, which is precisely the situation this document
exists to prevent.

## Sublicensing and other licence terms

The licence includes **the right to grant sublicences**, in all the fields of
exploitation above, including:

- under GPL-3.0-or-later, which is how the project is released today,
- under other open terms, for example Apache-2.0 or MIT,
- under closed or commercial terms, should the Licensee choose to offer them.

This does not mean the project will change licence. It means such a decision
will not be blocked by a single contribution made years earlier.

the parties exclude the right to terminate it.

Should the licence nevertheless be terminated or expire, that does not affect:

1. copies and versions of the project already distributed,
2. sublicences already granted to third parties, which remain in force on their

3. rights anyone has acquired under GPL-3.0-or-later.

The last of these follows from the GPL itself and is not altered here:
**whatever was released under the GPL stays under the GPL forever.**

## What this document does not do

- **It does not transfer copyright.** You keep it in full. This is a licence, not
  an assignment and not a waiver.
- **It does not take your contribution away from you.** You may use, publish and

- **It does not change the licence of versions already released.**
- **It does not cover trademarks.** The "Open Radio" name and marks are outside
  both this document and the project licences — see `TRADEMARKS.md`.
- **It creates no employment, no partnership and no obligation to accept a
  contribution.**

## Your statement, and opting out

By contributing you state that you are entitled to grant the licence above, that
the contribution is your own work or that you hold the necessary rights, that it
infringes no third-party rights, and that if it arose in connection with
employment or a commission you have the consent of whoever holds the rights.

If you would rather not grant this licence, say so plainly in the pull request.
Small fixes, documentation, translations and catalog data can usually be
accepted anyway; for larger code changes the maintainer will decide case by case.
There is no automatic refusal to work with you.
