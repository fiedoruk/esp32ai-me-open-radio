# Support

[Polska wersja](SUPPORT.pl.md)

Public releases 0.1, 0.2, 0.2.1, 0.4, 0.4.1 and the current 0.4.2 are live;
official binaries come only from `fiedoruk.pl/os/`. 0.4.2 is validated on one
reference device, a Core2 v1.3; 0.2.1 remains the release with the widest
hardware evidence. Reports go
to the issue tracker at [github.com/fiedoruk/open-radio](https://github.com/fiedoruk/open-radio);
security reports follow `SECURITY.md`.

## Supported reports

Bluetooth compatibility results, station playback results and sanitized
callback traces. Do not include Wi-Fi credentials, SSIDs, endpoint URLs,
Bluetooth addresses, serial logs, device identity, listening history, PCM or
third-party artwork.

## Boundaries

Security reports follow `SECURITY.md` and use the private channel named there.
Custom builds, unsupported codecs, LE Audio-only speakers and non-Core2 boards
remain outside the support contract. A community result applies only to the
declared firmware hash and capability class.
